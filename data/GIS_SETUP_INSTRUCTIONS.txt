INDIA POLITICAL BOUNDARY GIS CONFIGURATION
==========================================

Place your official India Boundary vector file here:
Target Path: india_boundary.geojson
Supported Formats: GeoJSON (.geojson), ESRI Shapefile (.shp), GeoPackage (.gpkg)

Recommended Official Sources:
1. Survey of India (Official Portal: https://surveyofindia.gov.in/)
2. Bharat Maps / National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC / ISRO Bhuvan)
3. Natural Earth Data (Admin 0 - Countries)

Ensure the boundary file contains accurate EPSG:4326 (WGS84 Lat/Lon) coordinates.
Configure your satellite image bounding box in config.py (DEFAULT_GEO_EXTENT) to match your SatDump capture.
